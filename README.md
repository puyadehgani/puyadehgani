## Research & Academic Activity

My clinical and academic work focuses on **rhinology and endoscopic surgery**, with particular interest in chronic rhinosinusitis, advanced endoscopic sinus surgery, lacrimal pathway disorders, endoscopic dacryocystorhinostomy (DCR), olfactory dysfunction and sinonasal disease.

I am involved in **clinical research, international scientific collaboration and medical education**, with publications in peer-reviewed journals and participation in international rhinology initiatives.

## Naso Sano

### Founder & President — Naso Sano

**Naso Sano** is a non-profit initiative dedicated to scientific education, professional collaboration and the dissemination of knowledge in rhinology and related fields.

Its educational activities include international collaborations, scientific meetings and **Naso Sano Live Grand Rounds**, bringing together specialists and international faculty for accessible medical education.

## Facial Regenerative Medicine

Developing clinical and scientific activity in **Facial Regenerative Medicine**, with particular interest in autologous regenerative approaches, including **Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP)** and regenerative strategies for facial tissue quality and restoration.

## Scientific & Educational Mission

My professional interests combine **clinical practice, surgery, research and medical education**, with the aim of promoting evidence-based rhinology and facilitating international scientific exchange.
